# Privacy Policy for FitBMI Pro

**Effective Date:** May 30, 2026

Zentriq Studio ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use the FitBMI Pro mobile application (the "App").

By using FitBMI Pro, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

FitBMI Pro is designed to respect your privacy. Because we use anonymous authentication, **we do not collect personally identifiable information (PII)** such as your name, email address, or phone number. 

However, to provide our core features, we collect the following types of data:

### A. Health and Fitness Data
To calculate your Body Mass Index (BMI) and provide health insights, you voluntarily input:
* Height
* Weight
* Daily calorie intake (optional)
* Daily water intake (optional)

### B. Automatically Collected Data & Device Information
When you use the App, certain information is collected automatically by third-party services integrated into the App:
* **Anonymous User IDs**: We generate a unique, anonymous identifier for your device to save your BMI history.
* **Device Information**: Device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers (including Google Advertising ID).
* **Usage Data**: Information about how you interact with the App (e.g., screens visited, buttons clicked, crash logs).

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:
* **To Provide Core Functionality**: Your height, weight, and related inputs are used locally to calculate your BMI and determine your ideal weight range.
* **To Sync Your History**: Your health inputs are securely backed up to our cloud database using your anonymous ID so you can view your progress in the History tab.
* **To Serve Advertisements**: We use third-party advertising networks (Google AdMob) that use device identifiers to serve relevant ads within the App.
* **To Improve the App**: We analyze crash reports and usage data to fix bugs and improve performance.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

FitBMI Pro utilizes trusted third-party service providers that may collect information used to identify you. The App uses the following third-party services, and we recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies:

* **Google Analytics for Firebase / Firebase Crashlytics**: Used for anonymous app analytics, crash reporting, and securely storing your health history.
  * [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* **Google AdMob**: Used to serve banner and interstitial advertisements. AdMob may collect and use your device's Advertising ID and IP address to provide personalized or non-personalized ads.
  * [Google AdMob Privacy Policy](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543?hl=en)

---

## 4. Data Security & Storage

Your health data (BMI history) is stored securely using Google's Firebase Firestore database. All data transmitted between your device and Firebase is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols. Because your account is strictly anonymous, this data is not linked to your real identity.

While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy

FitBMI Pro is intended for use by general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or higher age thresholds required by local laws). Additionally, BMI calculations are generally designed for adults. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete the data.

---

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

* **Advertising**: You can opt out of personalized advertising by going to your Android device settings: **Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization** (or "Delete advertising ID").
* **Data Deletion**: Because your data is tied to an anonymous installation ID rather than an email address, clearing the App's data from your device settings or uninstalling the App will effectively sever your connection to the stored history.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

# Privacy Policy for FitBMI Pro

**Effective Date:** July 26, 2026  
**Last Updated:** July 26, 2026

Zentriq Studio ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is processed when you use the **FitBMI Pro** mobile application (the "App").

By using FitBMI Pro, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

### 1. Information We Collect and Storage Method

FitBMI Pro is designed as a **privacy-first, offline-capable application**. We do not require account creation, registration, or login.

#### A. Health and Fitness Data (Local Storage Only)
To provide core features (such as calculating Body Mass Index, estimating daily calories, and tracking goals), you may voluntarily input:
* Height
* Weight
* Age and Gender
* BMI history and target weight goals
* Profile names created for multi-user family tracking

> **Important:** All health, fitness, and profile data you enter is stored **locally on your device** using an encrypted local database. **We do not transmit, collect, or store your health data on external or remote servers.**

#### B. Automatically Collected Non-Personal Data
To ensure app stability and support free access via advertising, the App uses third-party services that may collect anonymized device identifiers:
* **Google AdMob:** Serves banner and interstitial advertisements. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* **Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics:** Collects anonymized usage stats and crash logs to help fix bugs. [Firebase Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

---

### 2. Data Retention Policy

* **Local User Data:** Because all health metrics, profiles, and BMI records are saved exclusively on your device, your data is retained on your device for as long as you keep the App installed or until you choose to delete it.
* **Server Data:** We do **not** collect, host, or store any personal or health data on external servers. Therefore, we do not retain any user data on our end.

---

### 3. Data Deletion Process

Because we do not store your data on external servers, you have full control over your data directly on your device:

1. **In-App Record Deletion:** You can delete individual BMI records at any time by navigating to the **History** screen and swiping left on any record. You can also delete user profiles from the **Select Profile** screen.
2. **Complete Data Deletion:** You can permanently delete all app data, profiles, and history at any time by performing either of the following steps:
   * **Clear App Data:** Open your device's **Settings** > **Apps** > **FitBMI Pro** > **Storage** > tap **Clear Data** (or **Clear Storage**).
   * **Uninstall App:** Uninstalling FitBMI Pro from your device will automatically and permanently remove all locally stored database files.

*Since no user data is stored on remote servers, no formal data deletion request to Zentriq Studio is required to erase your data.*
